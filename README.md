# FAO-GLEAM-dashboard
The Global Livestock Environmental Assessment Model (GLEAM) is developed by FAO for the environmental assessment of livestock agrifood system. Its public results are currently served through two separate Shiny applications: the GLEAM Emission dashboard and GLEAM Water dashboard. Both use 2015 as reference year. 
GLEAM dashboards are among FAO’s most widely used dashboards, attracting over 1,500 users per month.
The GLEAM team plans to merge the two into one dashboard. Rather than designing it only in-house, we propose to open the design question to young data and design talent who already use GLEAM. This serves three goals at once: better user-centred design, a stronger and younger GLEAM Network, and visible proof of GLEAM’s commitment to openness, in line with the AGPL-3.0 open-source licence adopted for GLEAM.
# Objective
Co-create, with young GLEAM users, the design of a single, user-centred GLEAM dashboard for livestock emissions and water use, while expanding the GLEAM Network.
